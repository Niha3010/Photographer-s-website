Get to know us

Behind the camera.

We are Niha and Damjan, two photographers based in Vienna. We love capturing real moments, beautiful places and the little details that make every story special.

Our story

Every picture tells a story.

Photography is more than just taking pictures for us. It is a way to save memories and show the world from a different perspective.

We enjoy exploring different styles of photography, including portraits, celebrations, landscapes and everyday life. Every project gives us a chance to learn something new and be creative.

Based in Vienna, we find inspiration in the people we meet, the places we visit and the moments we share.
